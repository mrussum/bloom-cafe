## 2. THE GAME — BLOOM

### What It Is
A cosy mobile merge game for iOS and Android. Free to download, ethical monetisation.
The player runs The Fluffy Bunny Café on a quaint Cambridge lane called The Croft.
Core mechanic: drag-and-drop merge grid — combine ingredients to create dishes, unlock stories, expand the café.
No energy timers. No manipulative ads. No fake scarcity. A game that respects the player.

### Why It Exists
Inspired by the viral Cakez77 / Tangy TD moment (March 2026) — a developer who checked
his Steam sales live on stream after 4 years of solo dev and earned $31,942 in 30 hours.
Michael wants to replicate that moment with a more compelling human story behind it.
The difference: two friends building a game for a real family mission, with a launch moment
engineered to go viral — checking downloads live, with the full story already told to the audience.

### Platform
iOS + Android (React Native + Expo)
Possible PC port later — Godot, targeting Steam Cooking Fest or Next Fest

### Monetisation (Ethical)
- Free to download
- Remove All Ads: £2.99 one-time, permanent, always easy to find
- Cosmetic Café Themes: £1.99 each
- Rowan Outfits: £0.99 each
- Story DLC (post-launch): £1.99–£3.99
- NO energy systems, NO pay-to-progress, NO manipulative timers

### Ownership Structure
- Michael Russum: 50% (technical lead)
- Annie Scholfield: 50% (creative director)
- Rin: 33% reserved — activates on joining, reverts 12 months post-launch if unused
- On Rin joining: all three hold ~33.3% each (Michael and Annie dilute equally)
- Annie has been told about Rin's reserved third — full transparency

## 3. THE WORLD — THE FLUFFY BUNNY CAFÉ

### Setting
The Croft — a quaint, cobbled lane in Cambridge, UK. Not quite tourist Cambridge,
not quite locals-only. Window boxes, a noticeboard always three months out of date,
one contested parking space that has caused a cold war.

### The Fluffy Bunny Café
Corner unit, two bay windows, original 1980s tiles Linda refused to replace.
Bunny motifs on everything — some tasteful, some emphatically not.
Chalkboard menu Rowan updates daily. Smells of coffee and something baking.
The kind of place that makes people slow down.
The family loves bunnies — this is a real family detail woven into the game.

## 4. THE RECIPE TREE

### Tier Structure
- Tier 1: Base ingredients (spawned, not merged) — 10 items
- Tier 2: Prepared basics — 8 recipes
- Tier 3: Café staples — 8 recipes
- Tier 4: Signature dishes — 6 recipes (each tied to a character story beat)
- Tier 5: Special occasions — 3 recipes
- Tier 6: Legendary — 2 recipes (the game's emotional climax dishes)

### Key Story Recipes
- **Mum's Friday Pizza** (T4): Rowan makes from memory. Linda: "You remembered the basil order."
- **The Name Day Bun** (T4): Linda made this the day Rowan told her his name.
  She left it outside his door without knocking.
  Dialogue: "Of course I did. You'd told me your name."
  — THE most important moment in the game. Quiet, specific, true.
- **Brigadier's Saffron Buns** (T4): He arrives on the windowsill before the timer goes off.
- **Trixie's Lavender Shortbread** (T4): She makes it alone. It works.
  Rowan tries it, says nothing for a long moment, then: "You made that."
- **The Golden Bun** (T6): The café's signature. Seasonal. Queue by 7am.
  Brigadier supervises from the wall. Alo shows Linda the write-up on his phone.
- **Linda's Table** (T6): Linda sits at an actual table in her own café for the
  first time in years. Rowan brings the food and sits with her. No fuss.
  The game's emotional climax. The camera slowly widens to show the whole café —
  warm, full, alive.

## 6. TECH STACK

### Mobile (Primary)
- React Native + Expo (TypeScript, strict mode)
- Zustand with AsyncStorage persistence
- Supabase (cloud saves + anonymous auth)
- RevenueCat (IAP)
- expo-haptics (merge feedback — CRITICAL for feel)
- react-native-gesture-handler (drag/drop)
- expo-av (ambient sound + SFX)
- expo-linear-gradient (UI polish)

### Architecture Rules (NEVER VIOLATE)
1. src/game/ = pure TypeScript ONLY — zero React imports, ever
2. All game logic is immutable — functions take state, return new state
3. Components never modify state directly — always via Zustand store actions
4. Merge engine returns null for invalid moves, never throws
5. Cloud save is fire-and-forget — never block gameplay waiting for it
6. Recipes are data, not code — defined in recipes.ts as a plain array
7. NPC dialogue is data — defined in npcs.ts, never hardcoded in components

### Folder Structure
```
bloom-cafe/

      mergeEngine.ts  ← Core merge algorithm
